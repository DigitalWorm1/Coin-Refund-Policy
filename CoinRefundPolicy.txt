### 1. Introduction

This Refund Policy explains how refund requests relating to Coin Group Savings are handled.

Coin Group Savings is a group savings management and recordkeeping application developed and published by DartCore Systems Ltd ("DartCore", "we", "our" or "us").

This policy applies to payments made directly to DartCore for paid digital features, subscriptions, service plans or other optional services that we may introduce.

It does not make DartCore responsible for money exchanged between members, group contributions, loans, repayments or transactions processed by independent financial service providers.

Nothing in this policy limits any consumer rights that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

### 2. Payments Covered by This Policy

Depending on the services made available in your country, payments covered by this policy may include:

- Paid app features or upgrades.
- Premium or subscription plans.
- Group administration or management plans.
- Optional digital services supplied directly by DartCore.
- Other services for which DartCore is the identified seller or merchant.

The payment screen or checkout page will identify the applicable service, price, payment method and any recurring charges before purchase.

### 3. Payments Not Directly Controlled by DartCore

The following payments are generally outside DartCore's direct refund process:

**Group contributions and savings:** Contributions paid or owed to a savings group belong to the relevant group arrangement and must be handled under its constitution, rules and applicable law.

**Loans and repayments:** Loan disbursements, principal repayments, interest, penalties and other obligations between group members are governed by the relevant group agreement and applicable financial arrangements.

**Third-party payment transactions:** Payments processed by banks, mobile money operators, payment gateways or other independent providers may be subject to their own refund and reversal procedures.

Where a transaction involves a third-party provider, we may help identify the relevant transaction reference or direct you to the appropriate provider, but we cannot guarantee that the provider will approve a reversal.

### 4. Circumstances in Which a Refund May Be Granted

A refund may be considered where:

- You were charged more than once for the same purchase.
- A payment was successfully collected but the purchased service was not activated or made available.
- DartCore was unable to deliver a paid service within the agreed period.
- A technical error caused an incorrect charge.
- You were charged after a valid cancellation of a recurring subscription.
- A purchase was made without your authorization, subject to appropriate verification.
- You qualify for a refund under applicable consumer protection legislation.
- DartCore determines that a refund is appropriate after reviewing the circumstances.

Each request will be assessed fairly and in accordance with applicable law.

### 5. Cooling-Off and Statutory Consumer Rights

Where applicable, consumers may have statutory cancellation or cooling-off rights.

For example, Zambia's Electronic Communications and Transactions Act, 2021 provides a seven-day cooling-off period for certain electronic service transactions, subject to statutory exceptions. It also provides a separate cancellation remedy where specified online transaction disclosure or ordering requirements are not met.

Some transactions, including certain financial services and fully performed services supplied with the consumer's consent before the cooling-off period expires, may fall within statutory exceptions.

The availability of a statutory right depends on the transaction, the consumer's location and the applicable law.

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove or reduce a consumer's mandatory statutory rights.

### 6. Subscription Cancellations

If paid subscriptions or recurring plans are introduced:

- The applicable billing frequency and price will be displayed before purchase.
- Users may cancel future renewals using the available cancellation method.
- Cancellation normally prevents future charges but does not automatically refund a previous valid payment.
- Access to paid features may continue until the end of the paid billing period, unless otherwise stated or required by law.
- Charges made after an effective cancellation may qualify for review and refund.
- Refunds for purchases made through Google Play or another app marketplace may need to be requested through that marketplace, subject to its policies and applicable law.

Deleting the App does not automatically cancel a subscription.

### 7. Duplicate, Failed and Pending Payments

If you believe a payment was duplicated, incorrectly processed or left pending:

1. Check the transaction status with your payment provider.
2. Retain the transaction reference and payment confirmation.
3. Contact us with the relevant details.
4. Allow reasonable time for verification with the relevant provider.

A failed payment should not be treated as a completed purchase merely because an amount temporarily appears as deducted or pending.

Where a payment was collected but the corresponding service was not supplied, we will investigate and take appropriate corrective action, including a refund where required or justified.
